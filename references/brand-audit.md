## Reconstruction boundaries

The jar is a proportional reconstruction from photographs, not manufacturing CAD. No physical measurements were supplied. It preserves the broad square body, rounded corners, glass neck and base, natural cork, seal and front label.

The front label was redrawn for legibility from the supplied references. It retains **0,5 g** and **PACKED IN GERMANY** from the actual product photograph; those are packaging text, not a newly invented provenance claim. There was no editable production label file. The olive seal follows the supplied cinematic screenshots; the documentary photograph looks more blue-grey under its lighting.

The site makes the distinction between the pictured **0.5 g package** and the current **1 g commercial offer** explicit. No fictitious harvest numbers, company history, health claims, test results, prices or certificates were added.

Fonts: Cormorant Garamond and Manrope from Google Fonts, self-hosted with OFL licenses supplied beside this file.
